Zůstaňme v kontaktu.

Měkké neTiming

Redakční doporučení · Updated 2026-11-09

An open door, not a commitment. It can mean genuine long-term interest, interest that is waiting for a specific signal, or a gentle no. A precise request for a particular update is a stronger signal than a warm but generic sign-off.

Co řekli

Let's keep in touch.

Also phrased as: “Keep us posted” · “Let's stay in touch” · “Keep me updated on your progress” · “Add me to your investor updates”

Co to může znamenat

Several readings are plausible. Context decides — not the words alone.

  • Interested, but waiting for a signal — a milestone, a lead investor or momentum in your round.Clues: They say what they'd like to see, ask when you expect to close, or ask who else is involved.
  • Genuine long-term relationship interest without a current decision.Clues: They ask to be added to your investor updates, reply to them, or offer help unrelated to investing.
  • A polite soft no that keeps the relationship pleasant.Clues: No specific request, no question about timing, and the phrase ends the conversation rather than opening a next step.

What strengthens or weakens the signal

Co signál posiluje

  • They ask for a specific update (a metric, a launch, a hire).
  • They propose a check-in at a defined point.
  • They reply to or comment on your updates.
  • They make an introduction without being asked.

Co signál oslabuje

  • Generic warmth with no question about your business.
  • No acknowledgement of later updates.
  • It follows a meeting where they did most of the talking about the fund rather than asking about you.

Na co se zeptat

Happy to. What development would be most useful for me to share — and would an update after [milestone] be helpful?

Co udělat dál

Treat them as outside your active round unless they say otherwise. With their agreement, add them to a concise investor update and share material progress. Don't send weekly check-ins; send high-signal updates.

Načasování follow-upu

Send one short reply now. Afterwards, update when something material happens — a monthly or quarterly cadence is a common upper bound for unsolicited updates.

Příklad (hypotetický)

Hypotetické — not a real company or investor.

Hypothetical: after a first call, an angel says “let’s keep in touch” and asks when the founder expects to launch the paid tier. That specific question tilts toward the “waiting for a signal” reading. The founder confirms the launch month, adds the angel to the quarterly update with permission, and sends the update with launch results.
