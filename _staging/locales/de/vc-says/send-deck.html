Schickt mir euer Pitch Deck.

MehrdeutigProcess

Redaktionelle Einschätzung · Updated 2026-11-09

Neutral on its own. It is usually an initial screening step and sometimes a sign of real early interest; what matters is what it follows and what happens after. Send a concise, current deck promptly and propose a specific next step.

Was gesagt wurde

Send me your deck.

Also phrased as: “Can you send over your deck?” · “Send me your pitch deck” · “Happy to take a look at the deck” · “Share some materials”

Was es bedeuten könnte

Several readings are plausible. Context decides — not the words alone.

  • Initial screening — a low-cost way for the fund to filter many companies.Clues: Asked at an event or by email without a conversation; often by an analyst or associate; no next step mentioned.
  • Genuine preliminary interest after a good conversation.Clues: It follows specific questions about your business; they mention who else will review it or suggest a call after reading.
  • A polite way to end a conversation — or market mapping for a thesis the fund is exploring.Clues: Very broad interest in your sector rather than your company; no follow-up questions after you send it.

What strengthens or weakens the signal

Was das Signal stärkt

  • They ask specific follow-up questions after reading.
  • They propose a call or name the next reviewer.
  • They request additional material (data room, model, references).

Was das Signal schwächt

  • No acknowledgement after you send it.
  • The request came with no context about your company specifically.
  • They ask for highly sensitive data before any conversation.

Was Sie fragen können

Of course — I'll send it today. After you've had a look, would a 20-minute call to go through questions make sense?

Nächster Schritt

Send a concise, up-to-date deck within a day, with a three-to-four-line summary in the email body and a proposed next step. Share only what you are comfortable sharing at this stage.

Timing des Follow-ups

Send within 24–48 hours. If there's no reply, one short follow-up after about a week, ideally with a small, real update.
