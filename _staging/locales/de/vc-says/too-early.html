Für uns seid ihr noch in einer zu frühen Phase.

Weiches NeinStage

Redaktionelle Einschätzung · Updated 2026-11-09

Usually a stage or evidence gap, sometimes a polite pass. The difference shows up in whether the investor names a specific milestone and invites an update — so ask for that milestone, keep raising from earlier-stage investors, and come back only with real progress.

Was gesagt wurde

You're too early for us.

Also phrased as: “It's a bit early for us” · “Come back when you have more data” · “We usually come in a little later” · “We'd like to see more progress first”

Was es bedeuten könnte

Several readings are plausible. Context decides — not the words alone.

  • Stage or cheque-size mismatch: the fund typically invests later, or writes cheques too large for your round.Clues: Their recent deals are mostly later-stage; they mention minimum revenue, round size or ownership targets; the feedback is about how the fund works rather than about your company.
  • A specific risk they want retired first — customer demand, retention, a technical proof point or a key hire.Clues: They name a metric or milestone, ask detailed questions about that area, or ask you to send an update when you reach it.
  • A polite decline with no real plan to reconsider.Clues: No milestone, no timeframe and no request for updates; a one-line reply after a long silence; the fund does invest at your stage but offers no specifics.

What strengthens or weakens the signal

Was das Signal stärkt

  • They name a concrete milestone (e.g. a revenue level, number of paying customers, retention cohort).
  • They ask you to send an update at a specific point.
  • They introduce you to an earlier-stage investor or offer feedback on the deck.
  • The person who said it is a decision-maker who spent real time on your business.

Was das Signal schwächt

  • No milestone, no timeframe and no request to stay in touch.
  • It arrives as a short reply after weeks of silence.
  • The fund has recently invested at your stage, which suggests the issue may be conviction rather than stage.
  • The wording reads like a template sent to many founders.

Was Sie fragen können

Thanks for the candid feedback. So I can focus on the right things — is there one specific milestone (customers, retention or something else) that would make it worth revisiting?

Nächster Schritt

Log the feedback and any milestone they mention. Check the fund's typical stage before drawing conclusions. Keep the round moving with pre-seed/seed specialists now, and re-contact this investor only when you have material progress against the milestone they named.

Timing des Follow-ups

Reply once within one or two business days to thank them and ask the clarifying question. After that, reconnect when you hit the milestone — not on a calendar reminder alone.

Beispiel (hypothetisch)

Hypothetisch — not a real company or investor.

Hypothetical: a seed-stage B2B software founder with three paying pilots pitches a fund whose recent deals are mostly Series A. The partner says “you’re too early for us” and mentions they usually want a longer revenue history. The stage-mismatch reading is the most plausible here. The founder thanks them, asks whether an update after two more quarters of revenue would be useful, and refocuses the round on seed specialists.
