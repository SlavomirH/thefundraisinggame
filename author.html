Why he wrote the book

"If there's one thing I've learned, it's that fundraising is like dating. Impressing someone at the start is simple, but proving you're worth sticking with takes work."

Watching founders prepare for investors, he saw the same pattern again and again: strong businesses with polished decks that still stalled after the first meeting. The founders who closed weren't always the ones with the best numbers. They were the ones investors wanted to work with. The book is his attempt to make those unspoken rules explicit.
