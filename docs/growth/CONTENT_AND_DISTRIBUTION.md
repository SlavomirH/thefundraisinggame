# Content and distribution execution plan — The Fundraising Game

## Audience, promise and funnel

**Core audience:** pre-seed to Series A startup founders, first-time fundraisers, and CEE/European founders fundraising with international VCs. Do not infer that they use any particular fund type or country unless supported by keyword research.

**Value proposition:** Understand investor behavior, navigate meetings, reply confidently, and build a realistic fundraising plan. Voice: experienced founder/VC operator; precise, practical, occasionally humorous, not manipulative or overconfident. Dating metaphor is an editorial layer, not a substitute for substance.

**Conversion sequence:** SERP/social/community -> relevant article/tool -> useful result -> related guide/tool -> existing email early-access form -> confirmed opt-in -> book launch updates and optional future original investor research.

## Days 15–30: compound discovery

- Validate candidate queries from `growth-assets/research/keyword_opportunities.csv` for UK, US and relevant EU/CEE markets. Keep countries/currencies separate.
- Publish 2–4 new articles only if they contain original viewpoints, useful examples or first-hand research absent from existing material.
- Test clearer CTAs on article/tool pages. Track both button click and confirmed signup, not just traffic.
- Produce a short, shareable breakdown for each tool using properly attributed visual content. Avoid synthetic claims like '80% of VC rejections mean X'.
- Secure a handful of genuine editorial placements or mentions with relevant accelerators and founder educators; measure referral sessions and leads.
- Start a simple founder email series after subscribers explicitly opt in, consistent with email privacy and consent requirements.

## Days 31–60: data-led optimization

- Identify top query/page combinations from Search Console. Improve actual pages receiving impressions, not arbitrary keywords.
- Extend tool functionality based on observed usage and feedback; do not add accounts or data collection just for perceived engagement.
- Publish 3–5 credible investor insights from permissioned recorded interviews; link to canonical article pages and source context.
- Experiment with highly specific European/CEE fundraising resources only where demand and accurate regional information are documented.
- Improve source attribution and technical quality (metadata, mobile performance, internal links) rather than adding low-value pages.

## Days 61–90: scale evidence of demand

- Create a 'VC Says vs. Means' content library from consented, fact-checked real interviews, classified by investor comment, possible explanations, evidence, founder next action. Avoid invented quotes or pretending investor psychology is universal.
- Group top-performing questions into an evergreen founder playbook; publish newsletter excerpts with backlinks to the original source.
- Evaluate localization with measured regional queries. If demand exists, plan a single thoroughly localized CEE-targeted asset, not bulk automatic translations.
- Review business metrics: organic entrants, return visits, tool completion rate, early-access conversions per 100 unique visitors and eventual book purchases if measurable.

## Distribution channels (ordered by effort/fit)

1. **Organic Google and Bing:** high-intent investor psychology, follow-up, meeting and fundraising math queries. Measured through Search Console and server/analytics data.
2. **Personal LinkedIn/founder network:** first-hand insights from actual coaching/interviews, one useful tool per post. No canned engagement bait or exaggerated social proof.
3. **Accelerators, incubators, startup communities:** offer a free, clearly useful tool, checklist or educational workshop. Any link must be editorially earned and contextually relevant.
4. **Podcast/newsletter guests:** share an original perspective (fundraising-as-dating + real investor research), never imply interviews or endorsements that haven't happened.
5. **AI answer discovery:** clear definitions, structured FAQs, sourceable original research, crawlable fast pages. Don't rely on `llms.txt` as a special ranking key.
6. **Localized CEE founder content:** only once actual demand and accurate region-specific facts are validated.

## Outreach micro-briefs for human review

- **Accelerator manager:** 'I built a free investor follow-up builder and signal decoder to help founders after pitch meetings. If any of your founders would find it useful, I'm happy to share the tools and collect feedback.'
- **Newsletter editor:** 'I’m collecting real investor perspectives on what "too early" and "keep us posted" mean in practice. I put a free reference and tool together; happy to share specific examples for your readers if relevant.'
- **LinkedIn:** Lead with the counterintuitive insight, give an illustrative example, note its limitations and link to the genuinely relevant page. Avoid falsely universal predictions.

## Growth KPI definitions

- Search impressions/clicks: Google Search Console, segmented by route/query/country; no assumed click-through benchmarks.
- Tool started: first intentional input interaction (once per page view).
- Tool completed: output successfully rendered after explicit action.
- Tool-to-signup click: click to functioning signup CTA.
- Signup success: actual email-provider success response, consent-respecting and deduplicated.
- Quality outcome: book preorders/sales only if genuinely available and attributable.

Prioritize ratios and engaged leads over article count or raw search impressions. A 90-day plan is an experiment horizon, not a guaranteed ranking deadline.
