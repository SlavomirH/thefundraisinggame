About fundraising

How do VCs make decisions?

Usually emotionally first, then rationally. A partner forms a view early, often in the first meeting, then uses diligence and the partner meeting to confirm or reject it. Read more on investor psychology.

What do VCs look for in founders?

Beyond traction and market size: trustworthiness, clear judgment, honesty about what they don't know, and conviction that the company will get built either way.

How should I pitch to investors?

Treat the first meeting like a first date: aim for a second meeting, not a close. Open with why you, leave room for questions, and follow up exactly as promised. See the full guide.

How do I raise seed funding?

Build a focused list of investors who back your stage and sector, get warm introductions where you can, and run a tight process over a few weeks so interest builds at the same time.

Is raising venture capital in Europe different?

The fundamentals are the same, but ecosystems are smaller and more connected, so reputation travels quickly. Relationships built before you raise matter even more.
